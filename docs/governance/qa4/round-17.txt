the CI command against a planted finding and require a non-zero exit. Add R17-BAN1 to the catalogue.

pyiceberg 0.12 retries a conflicting commit on top of the other writer's. The delete's parent is then not `head`,
and "rows" mixes the other writer's 7 rows into the count. The docstring claims counting "at the two named
snapshots, not at 'the table now'" for exactly this reason.

head (see Verified). No production code calls `delete_before`, so its `None` return is safe today.

**Fix:** count at the new snapshot's `parent_snapshot_id` (`table.snapshot_by_id(snapshot_id).parent_snapshot_id`),
not at the pre-load `head`. Assert `result.rows` in `test_delete_before_removes_strictly_earlier_rows`.
